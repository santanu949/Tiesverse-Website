About TIES

TIES is a forward-thinking, independent policy and research think tank that serves as a hub for youth-led intellectual engagement, shaping conversations from India to the global arena. Rooted in a distinctly Indian worldview, TIES brings together layered analysis and collective wisdom to address domestic and international policy, strategic affairs, emerging technologies, cyber studies, public affairs, sustainability, and ESG-related issues.

At the core of TIES is a dynamic team of over 200 young professionals and scholars representing a wide socio-economic and academic spectrum. Our members come from leading institutions such as Delhi University, IIMs, IITs, NLUs, Ashoka University, the University of Glasgow, the University of Alberta, Amity University Mumbai, and several other prestigious Indian and global universities. This diverse intellectual pool fuels TIES’ rigorous and multidisciplinary approach.

TIES’ initiatives reach a monthly audience of nearly one million. We publish youth-driven opinion pieces, deliver insightful infographics and creative analyses across digital platforms, and host structured monthly deliberation sessions on contemporary issues.

We remain equally committed to enhancing the capabilities of our members through regular workshops and training sessions on design, writing, research methodologies, and policy brief development.

TIES stands as a symbol of informed youth leadership—engaging, empowering, and elevating young minds to contribute meaningfully to public discourse and global understanding.
